A few good shouts for the weekend 👇

There is a lovely mix on around Helsinki and Espoo this Saturday and Sunday.

You could start small with Megamuskari, spend the day in costume at Museo Leikki, paint some old glass into something worth keeping, or head out to Aurora once it gets dark for lights, workshops and a free outdoor circus show.

Sunday has the final Helsinki performance of Circus Finlandia, plus a much gentler little forest concert for babies and younger children in Tapiola.

We have checked the times, prices, age guidance and booking details, but give the organiser’s page one last look before leaving home. You will find every link at expats.fi/events 🙏
